# CloudNest Billing Policy

## 2.1 Plans and Pricing
CloudNest offers three plans: Free, Plus and Pro.
The Free plan costs nothing and includes 5 GB of storage.
The Plus plan costs 199 rupees per month and includes 200 GB of storage.
The Pro plan costs 499 rupees per month and includes 2 TB of storage and priority support.
Paying yearly instead of monthly gives a 20 percent discount on Plus and Pro.

## 2.2 Payment Methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI and net banking.
Payments are charged automatically on the same date each month or year.
If a payment fails, we retry it three times over seven days before downgrading the account to Free.

## 2.3 Refund Policy
You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first paid purchase.
Refunds are not given for renewal payments, except when the charge was made in error.
To request a refund, email billing@cloudnest.example with your account email and invoice number.
Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

## 2.4 Upgrading and Downgrading
Upgrades take effect immediately and you pay only the prorated difference for the current period.
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
If your stored data is larger than the new plan's limit, uploads are paused until you free up space, but no files are deleted.

## 2.5 Cancelling a Subscription
Cancel anytime from Settings, then Billing, then Cancel subscription.
After cancelling, you keep paid features until the end of the period you already paid for.
The account then returns to the Free plan rather than being closed.
