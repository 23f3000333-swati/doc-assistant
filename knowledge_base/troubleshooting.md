# CloudNest Troubleshooting

## 3.1 Login Problems
If you forgot your password, click Forgot password on the login page to receive a reset link.
The password reset link expires after 1 hour, so request a new one if it stops working.
After five wrong password attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes for security.
If you lost your 2FA phone, log in using one of your saved backup codes.

## 3.2 Sync Not Working
First check that the desktop app shows a green tick, which means it is connected.
Pausing and resuming sync from the app menu fixes most stuck syncs.
Files with names containing characters like a colon or question mark cannot sync on Windows.
If sync is still stuck, sign out of the desktop app and sign in again.

## 3.3 Slow Uploads
Upload speed depends mainly on your internet upload bandwidth.
Uploading many small files is slower than one large file, so zip small files together first.
You can raise the bandwidth limit in the desktop app under Preferences, then Network.

## 3.4 Contacting Support
Free plan users can reach support by email at help@cloudnest.example and get a reply within 48 hours.
Pro plan users get priority support with live chat and a reply within 4 hours.
Include screenshots and your app version number to get help faster.
